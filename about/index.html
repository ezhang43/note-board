Why BusyAnts

  • Free, with no card limits

    Every feature is free. Add as many cards, checklists, columns and boards inside boards as you like.

  • Satisfying checklists

    Tick things off and they drop into Completed. Nest items, drag them between lists, and clear done items in one go.

  • Share a board by link

    Send a link, and everyone who opens it edits the same board with you. Changes show up in a second or two.

  • Works offline, installs like an app

    Once visited, BusyAnts opens without internet and saves when you're back online. Install it on your computer or phone.
