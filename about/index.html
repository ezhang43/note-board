Your boards are safe

  • Undo and redo

    Press Ctrl+Z (⌘Z on a Mac) to take back a change, even a deleted card. On a phone, Undo is in the bottom bar.

  • Version history

    BusyAnts saves your boards as you work and keeps the newest 100 versions, free. Look at one, then restore it.

  • Backups you can download

    Save every board in one file any time, or the open board as plain text. Restore from the file later.

  • Works offline

    Open it once online and it opens without internet. Changes you make offline are saved when you're back.

  • Saved in two places

    A copy stays on your device, and another is kept online with your Google account. Only you can see your boards, unless you share one.

  • Bring your notes in

    Import Markdown, text, web pages, Trello or Google Keep exports, and PDFs.

Works well on a phone

  • A layout made for small screens

    On a phone, your board fills the screen, with Undo, Redo, a big + and a ⋯ menu in a bar along the bottom.

  • Move things with a finger

    Pinch to zoom. Hold a checklist item for half a second to lift it, then drag it where you want it.

  • Installs like an app

    Add it to the home screen of an iPhone, iPad or Android phone, or install it on your computer, with its own icon and window.

No AI pushed on you

BusyAnts has no built-in AI. It won't summarize, rewrite or sort your notes, and nothing in the app nudges you toward any of that.

If you do use Claude, there is an optional connector. You set it up yourself on your own computer, and it reads or changes your boards only when you ask Claude to. BusyAnts works the same without it. What it handles is in the Privacy policy.

How it works

  1. Sign in with Google

    One click, any Google account. Your boards are saved online and on your device, so they open on every device you sign in on.

  2. Fill your board

    Add notes, checklists and containers, then drag them where you want them.

  3. Share it, if you like

    Press Share and send the link. Anyone who opens it and signs in with Google can plan on the same board, and changes reach everyone within a second or two.

Quick answers

Is BusyAnts free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no limit on how many cards, containers or boards you make, and no ads.

How do I get a board back?

Press Ctrl+Z (⌘Z on a Mac) to undo a recent change. For anything older, open Version history, which keeps your newest 100 versions, and press Restore this version. You can also download a backup any time.

Does BusyAnts use AI?

No. BusyAnts has no built-in AI. There is an optional Claude connector that you set up yourself on your own computer. It reads or changes your boards only when you ask Claude to.
