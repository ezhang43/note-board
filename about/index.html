How it works

  1. Sign in with Google

    One click, any Google account. Your boards are saved online, so they open on every device you sign in on.

  2. Fill your board

    Add notes, checklists and containers, then drag them where you want them.

  3. Share it, if you like

    Press Share and send the link. Anyone who opens it and signs in with Google can plan on the same board, and changes reach everyone within a second or two.

Quick answers

Is BusyAnts free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no limit on how many cards, containers or boards you make, and no ads.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once you've opened BusyAnts online on a device, it opens there without internet too. Changes you make offline are saved online when you're back.

How does sharing work?

Open a board, press Share, then Share this board, and send the link. Anyone who opens it and signs in with Google gets that board and can change everything on it. Only the person who shared it can turn the link off.
