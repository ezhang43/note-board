- 2026-11-17 (owner decisions, Eric, job calendar-hotkeys, answering the pull request's three questions). (1) **"Keep step, retry":** when a Ctrl+Z or Ctrl+Y (or the message's Undo) doesn't get through to Google (fetch fails, offline, a timeout, a 5xx), the step stays on its list in its place, with its number, so pressing the key again tries it again; the usual failure message shows. The step is dropped only when Google says the change can't be done (a 4xx such as 400, 403, 404 or 410), and a series only half put back (UNDO_PART, SERIES_FAILED) is dropped as before. This replaces the earlier "my call" (3) that every refused step was dropped. (2) **"Accept for now":** an undo puts back this session's saved copy of the event even if it was changed elsewhere since (in Google Calendar or on another device), overwriting that change; no check is made. (3) **"No guests":** Ctrl+D copies the event without its guests, as built.
- 2026-11-17 (job calendar-hotkeys, my calls for "Keep step, retry"). (1) A 401 (access ran out: a new token is fetched next time), 408 (timeout) and 429 (too many requests) count as not getting through, so the step is kept; every other 4xx drops it. No access at all (Google's window blocked or closed) also keeps it. (2) Presses already queued behind one that didn't get through send nothing and keep their steps too, so an older step never reaches Google before a newer one (two edits of the same event would otherwise end up wrong). (3) A redo that didn't get through after a new change had cleared the redo list stays gone, and nothing comes back after the panel was opened again. (4) The Calendar API's errors carry Google's HTTP status (`CalendarApiError`, the same "Calendar API: <status>" message as before).
