- 2026-11-23 Google Calendar partial-match search (owner request, 2026-11-23; worker calls, rule 4 exception): Google Calendar API's `q` parameter only matches whole words/tokens on real calendars, so partial matching combines Google's 5-year `q` search with a read-only per-session in-memory index (`listForSearch`: 2 years forward and 2 past years read a year at a time so recent past events aren't starved by an older year's volume, `maxResults=2500`, up to 2 pages per window) filtered client-side by `matchesSearch` (every whitespace-separated term must match as a case-insensitive substring in title, description, location, or guest email/displayName). Merged results stay deduplicated and capped at 125 soonest upcoming + 125 most recent past events (`mergeSearchResults`). The index is cleared in `forget()` on any event change, fresh panel open, disconnect, or account switch (which also reloads the panel for the new person); if the index fetch fails transiently while `q` succeeds, `q` results still show and the next search retries the index. `src/calendar/devServer.ts` now mirrors Google's whole-word `q` behavior so browser tests genuinely verify partial matching.
