- 2026-11-19 (owner decisions, Eric, job calendar-search: "for calendar, can you also add in a search functionality as well"). (1) **Range:** the whole main calendar, past and future, using Google's own event search (the `q` parameter on events.list, primary calendar, singleEvents=true); it matches name, description, place and guests, as Google does. (2) **Look:** a magnifier button ("Search events", with the same tooltip) in the calendar's top bar next to Today opens a search box at the top of the panel, with the keyboard in it. Results replace the Week / Month list while there is search text, as a list grouped by date, with date headings and event rows looking like the Week view's rows. Order: upcoming events first, soonest at the top, then past events, most recent first. Clicking a result leaves the search, goes to that day (in the current view) and opens that event's card, as clicking it in the Week view does. Escape, or the x in the box, closes the search and goes back to the week or month as it was. Searching runs about 300ms after typing stops, shows "Searching..." while waiting and "No events found" when there are none. A failure shows the usual calendar failure message. This applies on the phone layout too. (3) **Hotkey:** / with the keyboard in the calendar panel (not in a text field) opens the search box; it is in calendarKeys.ts and the Keyboard shortcuts panel. Ctrl+F stays the board's Find bar.
- 2026-11-19 (job calendar-search, my calls). (1) **Window and cap:** Google can only sort events oldest first and a search needs both past and future, so the app asks for the future (today to 5 years ahead, the soonest 125) and the past (the 5 years before today, one request per year, each followed through up to 4 pages of 250, keeping the most recent 125): at most 250 events in all, 6 read-only requests per search. A year with over 1000 matches would lose its newest ones (marked `simple:` in the code). (2) **Upcoming means today or later:** an event is under the day it starts; today's events (even ones already over) are in the upcoming part, earlier days are the past part. A many-day event is listed once, under its first day; an event on the boundary of two requests is listed once. (3) **Headings** read like the Week view's ("Tue 6 Oct"), plus the year when it is not this year, since the search reaches 5 years either way. (4) **Empty or spaces-only text** sends nothing and shows the normal week or month (there is no "text" to replace it). (5) **Escape** in the box or on its x button closes only the search (the panel stays); Escape with the keyboard elsewhere in the panel closes the panel as before. After closing, the keyboard goes to the magnifier button so the panel's keys still work. (6) **The search is view state:** not remembered, and gone when the panel is opened again. A result whose day's events do not load (a failure) just leaves the search and shows the usual note, with no card. (7) **No new Google permission or write:** search uses the same read access as the week; nothing is created, changed or deleted. The dev/test calendar now understands `q` (name, description, place, guest email or name, ignoring case) so the browser tests can check it. (8) The `/` key follows the other calendar keys: only with the keyboard in the panel, not in a text field, not while the event form or a question is open, and a card that is open closes when search opens.
