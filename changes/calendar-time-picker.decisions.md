- 2026-10-29 (call, recommended) The Start and End time boxes in the calendar's two forms are one shared component (`src/components/TimePicker.tsx`): a text box (`role="combobox"`) that opens a list of times every 15 minutes in a popover drawn on the page itself, not inside the card (the card scrolls and would cut the list off), in front of the overlay. No new package. The native `<input type="time">` is gone. Why: the owner said Chrome's own dropdown looked off-centre and asked for "our own time picker like google calendar".
- 2026-10-29 (call, recommended) The box shows "2:00 PM" (12-hour, with minutes, upper-case AM/PM), the way Google Calendar's own picker does and as the coordinator's brief spelled out, not the shorter "2pm" the panel's lists use. Why: a picker that lists every 15 minutes reads better with minutes always shown, and the brief gave "10:00 AM" as the format.
- 2026-10-29 (call, recommended) The End list starts at the first 15-minute slot after the start and shows each option's length: "15 mins", "1 hr", "1.5 hrs", "2.5 hrs"; lengths that aren't a whole or half hour read "1 hr 15 mins". A start off the grid (typed "4:10pm") makes the first end option "4:15 PM (5 mins)". When the end is on a later day than the start (full form) the End list is the whole day with no lengths, since a length from the start time alone would be wrong. A start at 11:45 PM leaves the End list empty (no list opens); the end can still be typed.
- 2026-10-29 (call, recommended) Typed times: "2:10pm", "2:10 PM", "2:10p", "14:10", "9" (9:00 AM), "9am", "930", "1410", "12am" (midnight), "12pm" (noon). A bare hour is read on the 24-hour clock, so "9" is 9 AM and "13" is 1 PM. Anything else (including "24:00", "13pm", "9:5") goes back to the previous value, silently. It is taken on Enter or when the box is left; Enter then does not also save the form. Enter in a box that has no typing and no open list still saves, as before.
- 2026-10-29 (call, recommended) Escape closes only an open list: the form's Escape handler asks the open list first (`closeTimeList`), and does nothing more on that press. The list opens on focus, so Tab into the box opens it too. Enter picks the highlighted time only after ↑ / ↓ moved it, or takes what was typed; with neither it saves the form, as in any text box (a review finding: otherwise a time off the 15-minute list, such as 4:10 PM, was silently rounded to 4:15 just by pressing Enter). The list follows its box when the window resizes (a phone's keyboard) or something scrolls.
- 2026-10-29 (call, recommended) The list opens under the box, or above it when there is more room above (a phone's bottom sheet), at most 240px tall and 220px or the box's width wide. Rows are 32px on a mouse and 44px (16px text) on a phone-sized or touch screen.
