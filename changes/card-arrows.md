## Arrows between cards and containers (2026-11-03)
- Hover a card or container to see a small dot on each side. Drag a dot onto another card or container to draw an arrow: a thin gray straight line with an arrowhead. On a phone, tap a card first, then drag one of its dots.
- Arrows follow their cards when they are moved, resized, folded up, or moved into or out of a container. A card hidden in a folded container points from the container instead.
- Click an arrow to select it (it turns amber). Delete, Backspace or its small × button removes it. Ctrl+Z undoes drawing and deleting.
- Deleting a card deletes its arrows too, in the same undo step. Copy and paste (and Ctrl+D) copy only the arrows between the copied cards. The same two cards can't be joined twice.
- Arrows are saved with the board and go wherever it goes: sync, editing together, backups, Version history, shared boards and the Claude connector. Download as text leaves them out.
- **Boards already saved:** they open exactly as before, with no arrows. The first save after this update writes them as saved version 5, with nothing else changed. A copy of the app from before this update that is still open in another tab, or cached, can't read version 5. It leaves those boards alone instead of saving over them, as with earlier version changes. The old arrows that were removed on 16 Oct stay erased.
