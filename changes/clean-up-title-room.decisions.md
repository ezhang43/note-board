- 2026-10-24 Clean up never squeezes a list's title (owner request, job 208). It shares the title's line only when title, Clean up, arrow and trash all fit; otherwise it drops to its own line under the title, right-aligned in the title column, and the arrow and trash stay on the title line. Why: the title is what identifies the list. Done with a small wrapper around the title and Clean up in the list's header, because plain wrapping can't both keep Clean up left of the arrow and be the part that drops.
- 2026-10-24 (call, recommended) Side effects of that wrapper: the Tab order in a list's header is now collapse arrow, trash can, title, Clean up (Clean up used to come before the arrow); the comment chip sits to the right of Clean up; the empty "meta" space in a list header with Clean up is hidden so it can't take room from the title. Why: simplest markup that gives the layout the owner asked for, with no layout change for lists without Clean up.
