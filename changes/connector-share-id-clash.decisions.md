- 2026-10-23 Connector, which boards count as shared (job 192, fragile-connector F9): the connector now uses the app's own rule (`Sharing.ownCopy`), so the two agree. A share never takes one of the owner's own boards (anyone a share is shared with can save anything in its data), except, in a share of the owner's own, the board they shared and the boards inside it (never Home). The board they shared comes from the share document's `root` field, which the Firestore rules don't let anyone change, not from the share's data. A share whose starting board is one of the owner's ids holds nothing. The "just shared, old copy still in the own boards" case stays read-only.
