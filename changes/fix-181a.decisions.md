Calls made while building (recommended options, rule 4 exception): (1) **H1:** held changes are noted on the device as a fingerprint of the online boards they were made on (`note-board:held-edits:<uid>`, removed once an upload succeeds, or when the online boards turn out to have changed, and with the account's other data when it is deleted). A page opening on those same boards, as the server has them (in a marker or put back), keeps this device's copy and sends it once allowed; on a different online copy the usual "online wins on open" rule stands, so newer work elsewhere is never overwritten. Opened from the offline copy, the decision waits for the server's copy. (b), judging the marker's age by server time, was not done: it needs a server time on every save and a rules change, so it isn't small. Known limit: with two tabs held at once, a tab with no changes can save the marker's boards over the device copy after the other tab's changes, so those are lost as before. (2) **M1:** the member record's name must be the Google sign-in's own name (trimmed, or "Someone" when there is none) and its photo the sign-in's photo or none; `joinedAt` only the server's time, kept or set again (so no one sorts ahead of the owner). This stops copying the owner's name or photo through the database; someone who renames their own Google account can still take the same name, which is why the owner is also marked "Owner" by account (in People, where "Shared it" became "Owner", and on their comments). A Google name over 200 characters is refused rather than checked as cut (the rules can't take part of a text). A name that ends in an unusual space (such as a no-break space) that the app trims and the rules may not would be refused; not seen in practice.
