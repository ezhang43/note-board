- 2026-10-22 (owner picked option B, job 149, review 137-A H5): deleting an account first adds a marker to boards/{uid} (`deleting: 'started'`, the deleting page's `client`, `updatedAt`; the boards stay in it), and firestore.rules refuse board, version and shared-list saves under boards/{uid} while there is a marker; deletes still work. Order: shares, records, then the board is replaced by `{deleting: 'done', client, updatedAt}`, then version history and the list of shares, then the sign-in. My calls: while 'started', only the page that set it may change it (to put its boards back by syncing again if a later step fails, so that alone isn't "part-way"); a 'started' marker over 10 minutes old (the page closed part-way) lets any page save again (they wait from the marker's own time, plus 30 seconds). 'done' comes before the version history goes, so a version another page saved just before is deleted too, and it stays online for good with nothing of the person's in it, so a device reopened later (even offline with saves waiting) can never put anything back; any page of theirs can run deleting again from 'done' (only the sign-in failed). Other pages hold their changes ("Saving…") and show the boards in a 'started' marker; on 'done' they ask Google whether the sign-in is gone (a forced token refresh) and sign out with the note only if it is, so another tab of the same browser never signs out the deleting tab, and a page whose deleting stopped at the sign-in can still try again. Known limit: after a failed delete, signing out by hand on such a page shows the "deleted" note. Pages from before this change take the marker for their boards gone and sign out at once. The Privacy policy and Delete your account page mention the marker.
