## Deleting your account with another device open (2026-10-21)

- Deleting your account now deletes your own boards online first, so a phone or computer of yours that is still open stops syncing straight away. Before, it could see a shared board disappear first and save your boards and a version again just after they were deleted, leaving them online.
- Your own boards and version history are deleted once more just before the sign-in goes, to catch a version another device saved as it stopped.
