- 2026-11-11 Fold arrow, darker (call): the arrow uses `--ink`, the strongest ink color. Why: `--ink-soft` is almost the same as `--ink-faint`, so no other existing color is meaningfully darker, and no new colors are allowed. Contrast against the card is 16.8:1 in light mode. The old hover rule was removed: the resting color is already `--ink`, so there is no stronger step to hover to.
- 2026-11-11 Fold arrow, bigger (call): the open arrow is drawn 13px wide at Normal (8.8px glyph, about 35% more than before) and the folded one 5.5px (about 38% more), with a 2.1 stroke set in the checklist CSS rather than in the shared chevron icon, so other chevrons are unchanged. It grows with the item buttons' step (`--bb`, capped at 190% like them); the 110% cap from the gap fix is removed.
- 2026-11-11 Fold arrow, room (call, recommended): a checklist that has a top-level parent item gets left padding of `12.78 * step - 10` px on its item list (about 2.8px at Normal, up to about 14px at 190%). Why: the bigger arrow needs about 12.8 units of room left of the row, more than the card's 12px padding at big sizes; this is the simplest fix. Other choices: always padding every list (a visible shift for every checklist), or capping the arrow's size (it stays small, the owner's complaint). What it shifts: that list's rows move right by that amount; the card title and lists with no parent are unchanged. Cost: the rows slide a few pixels when the first parent item is made or the last one removed. Sub-item arrows stay inside their 32px indent (about 19px spare at Normal, 7.7px at 400%).
- 2026-11-11 Fold arrow gap (call): the folded ▸ sits about 5.6px (times the step) from the box, the open ▾ exactly 4px; the ▸ is narrower and centered on the same spot, so I did not shift it to even them out.
