Owner decision ("option A", for the place of an item's add-estimate clock that job saved-estimate-doneon left open): widen every item's hover strip by about 26px so a faint clock button sits with the drag grip and the trash can, opening the same estimate box notes use; it works on a phone too. Calls made while building, recommended options (nothing here touches saving, the connector or loading): (1) The clock is the strip's last button, at the card's edge, beyond the grip and the trash can, not next to the text: a parent's "0/2" count may reach into the strip (item-count-no-wrap), and the end nearest the text is where it lands. A sweep test over last-word lengths, at Normal and Huge text on desktop and phone, proves the count never covers the clock and never drops to its own line. The sweep only hits the worst case when a last word ends exactly at the text's edge, which passed on one machine's fonts and failed on the GitHub runner's (IBM Plex Sans there, a fallback font here), so a second test measures the room itself: the space between the text's right edge and the clock must hold a whole "0/12" count and its gap. On a phone (touch, under 600px) that room was 24px (the grip's strip) and the count needs about 35px, so it now grows with the text size (2.5 times the text's size, 40px at Normal; from 225% text up, with no clock, it stays 24px). Phone text is 16px narrower on every item at Normal text as a result (a leaf item can't be given a narrower strip than a parent: the text must wrap the same with or without a count). A count of 100 or more sub-items is wider and can still touch the clock; so can a count on a narrow window used with a mouse at Huge text. (2) The clock is a fixed 26px (not scaled by the text size step) and the strip is 26px wider on every item, with or without an estimate, so text wraps the same either way. (3) From the 225% text size up items get neither the clock nor the wider strip, as notes get no clock there. (4) On a touch screen the clock shows only while the item has the cursor, as the grip does, with a 44px press area that runs outward past the card's edge (the grip's now runs the other way, over the end of the row's text, since the clock sits beside it); on a touch screen that can also hover (tablet with a mouse) the grip's area stops at the trash can. (5) After Clear on an item the keyboard goes into the item's text (the pill is gone, so there is nothing else to return to). (6) No clock in view-only boards, published boards or exports (the same rule as the grip and trash can); they keep the wider strip so the text wraps the same as when editing. Selecting across items and deleting (`deleteRange` in `src/model/wordRange.ts`) now keeps an estimate that would otherwise have been dropped; it is not on the risky-file list and only keeps data, so the job stays low-risk. (7) Estimate box fixes, each reproduced by a failing test first: after Ctrl+Z inside the Minutes box the box follows the undone value; pressing the open pill again closes it and returns the keyboard to that pill; selecting from one item into the next and deleting keeps the upper item's estimate, else the lower's (the join rule).
