- 2026-10-29 Journal general notes, how they are recognized: by the note's first line being exactly "General notes" on the Journal board, the same way days are found by their date line. No new saved field, so sync, import, copy and older copies of the app all carry it as an ordinary note, and a date line never reads "General notes", so it is never taken for a day. If two exist (typed on two devices before they synced), the one with the smaller id is shown everywhere; the other stays an ordinary note.
- 2026-10-29 Journal general notes and the hidden rule: the Journal board stays hidden from the Boards menu while it holds only dated notes plus one general notes card. A second "General notes" card, or any other card, lists it as before.
- 2026-10-29 Journal general notes are made lazily, on the first typing that leaves text or formatting, never on opening the panel. That typing and the new card are one undo step on the Journal board, so one Ctrl+Z removes both.
- 2026-10-29 Journal general notes placement: the card goes to the right of everything on the Journal board, level with its top, on a free spot. Today's note ignores the general notes card when it works out "straight above, level with the leftmost", so it lands exactly where it did before.
- 2026-10-29 Journal general notes are not part of the groups, entry counts, word counts or the folded timeline; search, Download as text and Version history treat them as the ordinary note they are.
- 2026-10-29 Journal general notes fold: open by default and open again every time the panel opens, even if folded last time (view state, not saved or synced). Today's note keeps the cursor when the panel opens.
- 2026-10-29 Journal general notes look: a quieter sheet above today's page (card background, thin edge, no shadow), a 15px semibold "General notes" header button with the group headers' arrow, `aria-expanded`, a focus ring, and at least 44px tall on a phone; the empty text box shows "Notes to keep, not tied to a day…".
- 2026-10-29 Journal general notes, known limits (from the high review, left as they are): a note the owner already wrote on the Journal board whose first line is exactly "General notes" becomes the panel's general notes (if the board holds nothing else but days, it now leaves the Boards menu, and the first edit in the panel adds a blank line under that first line); nothing is lost. A copy of the general notes card made on the Journal board (Ctrl+D, paste) may take over from the original, since the smaller id wins and ids are random; the other stays an ordinary note with its text. Both are rare, and a timestamp field would be a new saved field for little gain.
