- 2026-11-20 Speed on big boards (job perf-fewer-elements): the hover-only icons on checklist rows (Move up / down, trash, grip, estimate clock) and on card and container headers (collapse arrow, trash, estimate clock, resize corner) are drawn by CSS (a mask on the button's ::after) instead of inline SVG, rather than adding or removing the buttons as the pointer moves. Why: it halves the board's elements with no change to look, hit areas, tab order or touch behavior, and no extra re-rendering on hover. The drawings are hand copies of the ones in icons.tsx (a `simple:` note there). The item fold arrow, the container's add button and the comment chip keep their SVGs (few of them).
