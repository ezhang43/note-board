(1) Saving: `WORKSPACE_VERSION` went from 5 to 6, as the job asked, because an older copy of the app would drop the new fields on its next save; versions 3 to 5 still load unchanged, an older copy refuses version 6 ("saved by a newer version… Reload") and never saves over it, and the Claude connector went to 1.7.0 (an older one says "Safe to edit: no"). (2) An estimate is whole minutes from 1 to 1440, the same cap as a brain dump's minutes; a loaded or imported value outside that, or a `doneOn` that is not a real "YYYY-MM-DD" day, is dropped and the rest of the item kept. (3) A `doneOn` left on an open item (written by hand or by another tool) is kept on load but counts for nothing; it is only read while the item is ticked. (4) The day is this device's local date, made where the tick happens (the app, or the connector's computer). Every way of ticking stamps it, including a parent ticked by its last open sub-item; a parent ticked again because its open sub-items were removed gets no day (a stale day left on it is removed), and ticks in the Habits panel get none. (5) Enter splitting an item keeps the estimate on the first half only, and a ticked item's day on both halves; joining two items with Backspace or Delete keeps the upper item's estimate, or the lower one's when the upper has none. (6) The connector sets a note's estimate only when adding it (`add_note`), since it can't edit notes. (7) Look and placement (owner may want to see it): a gray pill (the app's hover color, 12px, tabular numbers) at the end of an item's first line, before the hover buttons, and in a note's header; with no estimate a faint clock shows only on hover or keyboard focus, and on a touch screen only on the item being typed in (no button in the phone item bar). The picker is a small box under the pill with quick choices 15m, 30m, 45m, 1h, 2h, a Minutes box, Set and Clear; it fades in over 120ms (none with reduced motion), and closes on Escape, a press outside it, or focus leaving it. On an item the hover clock sits over the end of a long first line while shown, like the hover buttons beside it. A collapsed note has no clock, and from 225% text up no pill either: at 400% the clock pushed the trash button off a 200px collapsed note.
