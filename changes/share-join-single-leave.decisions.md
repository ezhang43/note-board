- 2026-11-11 (call, recommended) While this person's own leave of a shared board is on its way, the server saying they are off it is that same leave: no second leave is sent and no "no longer shared with you" note shows. "Was deleted by the person who shared it" still shows if the owner deletes it meanwhile, and a leave that fails still says "That didn't work" as before. A list of their shared boards sent before the leave doesn't open the board again; once the server's list has stopped naming it, or they open its link here, it is treated as usual (so being added back on another device still brings it here). Why: a flaky browser test showed join, leave, leave; this is the smallest fix that keeps every note the spec promises.
- 2026-11-11 (owner decision on pull request #269) A board whose own leave is still on its way isn't closed by the server's list of shared boards leaving it out; it waits for the leave, so a leave that fails changes nothing (the spec's "if it doesn't work nothing changes"). Every leave the shared-boards code sends (the Share panel, a damaged link, the one retry after a failed leave, the clean-up after being removed or the board being deleted) goes through one place. The person's own leave is sent once; the one retry is for a leave whose boards have already gone from here (as before, plus a damaged link's), and no retry goes out after signing out. The clean-up joins their own leave if one is on its way and is otherwise sent once, as before.
