## Leaving a shared board sends one leave (2026-11-11)

- Opening a link to a shared board that can't be used, or leaving a board someone shared with you, now tells the server once that you are leaving. Before, if the server said "you're off this board" before it had answered the leave itself, the app sent a second leave, and leaving from the Share panel could also show "no longer shared with you". An out-of-date list of your shared boards no longer opens a board you just left, either. Nothing else you see changes. If leaving doesn't work ("That didn't work"), the board now always stays open as before, even when the server's list of your shared boards already left it out. If you were taken off a board while your leave of it was still on its way and you open its new link, the board you reopen no longer closes by itself when that old leave answers (the old leave can still take you off it on the server, as before; and if you open the link before the board has gone, that leave still takes it away when it answers). If you are taken off a board while leaving it and open its new link, leaving it again sends a leave of its own, and being removed again shows "no longer shared with you".
