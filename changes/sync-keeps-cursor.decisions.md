- 2026-11-09 (job sync-keeps-cursor) The guard sits in the one keyboard handler every Delete / Backspace block delete goes through (`useShortcuts`), fed by `src/components/lostCursor.ts`: it notes the block text box the cursor is in and forgets it only when the person leaves it on purpose (a click or tap that moves the focus, Tab or another text box, or a key the app answers by taking the cursor out: Escape, an arrow onto a card with no text box, Ctrl+A in a list, pasting copied items). Done on the phone item bar counts as leaving on purpose too. A focus move within 0.6 s of the person's own press, drag, release or click counts as theirs (a tap moves the focus a moment after it is let go), unless the box was taken off the page. A checklist item's tick box and buttons are tracked like its text box. While the cursor has fallen out of that box any other way, any board key first lets the selected blocks go (as undo-keeps-cursor does when no item is left), so Delete and Backspace delete nothing and arrows nudge no block, for every press until a click, tap or focus move, so a held Backspace can't delete the block either. Ctrl+A and arrow keys pressed on the page don't turn it off, since someone who kept typing may press them too. The tracking listeners are started by the board's shortcuts and stopped with them. A Version history restore on this device needs no guard (it starts with a click, and lets the selection go already); one made on another device arrives as a sync.
