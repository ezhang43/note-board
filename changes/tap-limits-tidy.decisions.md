- 2026-11-03 Tap limits (follow-ups to job 224; worker calls, owner approved the follow-ups): the tick box tap's own numbers (10px, 500ms) meant the same as `DRAG_THRESHOLD` (5px: a press that moves further is a drag or pan) and `HOLD_MS` (450ms: a press that rests longer is a long press), which the zoom taps on the empty board already use through `pressKind`, so the tick box now calls `pressKind` with those two constants instead of its own. A tap is a bit stricter (5px, 450ms); a thumb that wobbles more than 5px was a pan on the board and now ticks nothing on the box too.
- 2026-11-03 Pinch edge case: reproduced with a two-finger touch test: a second finger anywhere while the first rests on a tick box, then the second lifting first, ticked the box (the box only counted the fingers left on screen at the lift). The box now listens for any non-primary pointer down during its press and treats the press as a pinch. The board's other taps (zoom taps, narrowing a selection) already cancel on a second finger; their tests were added as guards and passed unchanged.
