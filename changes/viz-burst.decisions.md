- 2026-11-13 Arrivals overlap by waves: a list of n items lands in waves of round(n/6) (at most 8), 120ms apart, one wave per (wave size times the usual gap). Up to about 8 items it stays one at a time. At most 8 beams and fountains are drawn at once (the oldest are cut short; every item still lands, lights its dash and joins the feed). Why: the old loop already allowed overlap but ran at about one a second, so it never looked busy; waves look like the second reference clip and the cap keeps the frame cost bounded.
- 2026-11-13 A fountain has 10 sparks, shared out so about 60 are in the air together (never under 4; about 70% on a stage under 520px wide). The count is worked out once per loop from the busiest moment, so no spark pops in or out. Why: the frame budget with up to 8 fountains at once; fewer sparks per fountain when crowded looks the same as a dense burst but costs much less.
- 2026-11-13 Lit cells show one dash per finished item at its own fixed place, never more than 40% of a cell's places (later items share an earlier place). Why: the disc must not turn into solid pink bands with a long list.
- 2026-11-13 Name cards: at most 2 on screen, 2.6 seconds each, and an arrival within 0.7 seconds of the last card gets none. They are drawn as plain text on the canvas (no HTML), and not at all under reduced motion. Why: a wave of arrivals would otherwise flicker the cards; plain canvas text cannot carry markup from item text.
- 2026-11-13 Camera: only a modest change (tilt 0.55 to 0.62, so the disc is taller). Why: the disc's width is held back by the names round the rim (from the rim-names job), so making it much larger would cut those names. The asked-for "larger and closer" is therefore only partly met; a much bigger disc would need the names moved or shortened, which the owner should decide.
- 2026-11-13 In a busy wave most items get no card (one card per 0.7 seconds at most, so about one in six when items land 120ms apart), and when a third card arrives it takes the first one's place while that fades out over 0.2 seconds, so the two briefly overlap. Why: a card per item would flicker and bury the disc; the brief overlap is a known limit, small enough not to need a third place.
