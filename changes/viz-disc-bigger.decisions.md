- 2026-11-13 The names stand above the disc's back and below its front, never beside it, and the disc is sized to the stage's width (the largest disc for which every name fits at every turn, worked out by `fitDisc` in `src/model/vizNames.ts`; the old width-reserve for the side names and `LABEL_RADIUS` are gone). Why: the owner said "make the disc bigger, move the names out"; beside the disc the names were what held its width back.
- 2026-11-13 A name whose wedge crosses the disc's far left or right edge hops from the back to the front, and fades out and back in over about 1.5 seconds each side of the swap (about 7 degrees of turn, at one turn per 80 seconds). Why: when the disc fills the width there is no room beside it, so the hop cannot be made continuous; a fade looks calmer than a jump. Known limit: a short name flickers out briefly twice a turn.
- 2026-11-13 Names that would touch are moved apart, up or down, so a crowded corner stacks them in a short column; they never overlap. Why: with up to 17 names this is the simplest rule that keeps every name readable.
- 2026-11-13 On a short stage or a narrow phone with long names the disc shrinks (steps of 7%, down to a floor of 40px of half width, where names may then touch on a tiny stage) until every name fits at 72 sample angles round a whole turn; it is checked on the pure model, so it never needs the browser. Why: "pick the largest disc that still keeps the names readable". Known limit: the sample is 72 angles, so a name could touch another by a pixel between samples on a very crowded stage.
- 2026-11-13 A name is cut (ending in …, keeping its first word) only if wider than the stage; before, it was cut whenever the old fifth-of-the-width reserve was too small. The tilt (0.62) is unchanged. The canvas publishes the disc's size as `data-disc` (middle x, middle y, half width, stage width, stage height) for the browser tests.
- 2026-11-13 Each item's sparks follow a path worked out by `flowAt` in `src/model/vizGeometry.ts` (pure, tested): they leave the cell, run inward on the disc's tilt along the cell's wedge (swinging sideways at most 0.4 radians and never out of the wedge), then lift off the hub into the core, slow at first and quickest at the end. Why: the owner asked for the energy to flow from the categories into the center; it replaces the old arc from the cell to the core. The same ten-per-item sparks and sixty-in-the-air cap apply, so the flow adds no particles.
- 2026-11-13 No steady idle flow along lit categories. Why: the reference's calm moments show only the discrete bursts and a still disc; a constant drift would add motion the reference does not have and more particles to the budget. Easy to add later (a few capped drifting sparks per lit wedge using `flowAt`) if the owner wants it.
- 2026-11-13 Arc height: the fountains' arc is `(0.2 + 0.3 * lift) * disc half width * sin(pi * q^0.8)` above the path (q is how far along the spark is, so the top comes about 2/5 of the way). That puts the top 0.075, 0.103 and 0.131 of the disc's width above the path for the lowest, middle and highest spark; I measured the reference at about 0.07 to 0.14 (mean about 0.10, the near cells rising about 0.25 of the width almost upright), and the old arcs at 0.021, 0.049 and 0.077 (the first flow version 0.012 to 0.032). The same ten sparks and sixty-in-the-air cap, so no more particles. Why: the owner asked for the arcs to match the video. Known limit: measured by eye from stills, so within about 0.02.
- 2026-11-13 Cards: four places (upper right, lower left, upper left, lower right), found by `cardRoom` / `cardSlotsFor` / `pickSlot` in `src/model/vizBurst.ts` (pure, tested). Each is held inside the same ring the rim names stay outside of, which is what guarantees no card touches a name; a card is cut to the room its place has (up to 240px). The gap is 0.3 seconds, the life 2 seconds, and a list of 8 or fewer gets a card for every item (`SMALL_LIST`). A new card takes the place of the oldest still on screen; the old one fades out in 0.15 seconds before the new one fades in. A place needs 80px of room, so a phone (and a small laptop window under 600px wide) uses only the lower pair; the cards there are narrow and their text is cut with an ellipsis. Why: the owner's request for cards on more items without overlap. Known limit: on a phone only two cards show at once, so in a long list items beyond that wait for a place (a new card takes the oldest's).
