- 2026-11-14 Variants without a fuzzy library: a run of three or more of the same letter shrinks to two or one ("sooon" to "soon", "nowww" to "now"), and two at the very end shrink to one ("yess"). One ASCII letter alone, or one letter repeated ("x", "aaa", "xx"), and text with no letters at all (digits, symbols, emoji, empty) count as empty. A single non-Latin character (such as a one-character word in Chinese) is not treated as empty.
