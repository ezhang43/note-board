- 2026-11-15 (owner choice) When the calendar fetch hits its 5-page cap, the events left out are the oldest, not the newest. Google can only list events oldest-first (`orderBy` is `startTime` or `updated`; there is no descending order), so when the first read of the whole range hits the cap the range is read again in 30-day pieces, newest piece first, until 5 extra pages are spent (a piece's first page is free, so quiet months cost nothing). A piece the pages cannot finish is left out whole (unless it is the newest), so what is kept is an unbroken run up to today. A calendar that fits in the cap costs the same one fetch as before; only a very full one costs up to about 30 more requests. Why: the recent days matter most in the Visualizer.
- 2026-11-15 (call, recommended) The Visualizer closes when the board goes away (a sign-out), by closing from an effect of the always-present Visualizer component after a zero-delay timer that React's mount, unmount, mount development check cancels. Why: the open flag is kept outside React and outlived the board, so the next sign-in in the same tab reopened it.
- 2026-11-15 (call, recommended) In Calm the Visualizer's newest-cell flash is not drawn at all, so the single picture drawn is the final one. Why: the flash fades over 0.7 s and would have been frozen half-faded once the loop stopped.
