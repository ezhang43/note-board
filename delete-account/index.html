Delete your account

You can delete your BusyAnts account and your data at any time, from inside BusyAnts (feedback you sent is kept apart: see below). It can't be undone.

In BusyAnts

  1. Open BusyAnts and sign in with the Google account you use for it.
  2. On a computer, open the File menu at the top. On a phone, press ⋯ at the bottom right, then File.
  3. Choose Delete account….
  4. If you want to keep a copy, press Download a backup first.
  5. Type DELETE, press Delete my account, and sign in with Google once more when asked, to show it's you.

Everything except feedback you sent is deleted straight away and you are signed out. (Only a small note that the account was deleted stays, with nothing of yours in it, so a device still signed in can’t put anything back.) You need an internet connection for this.

If you can't sign in

Email ezhang43@gmail.com from the email address of your Google account and ask for your BusyAnts account to be deleted. Everything, including feedback you sent, is deleted within 30 days of your request, and you get a reply when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • All your boards and everything on them.
  • Your version history (the earlier versions of your boards kept online).
  • Boards you shared, with the boards inside them: they are deleted for everyone you shared them with.
  • Your place on boards others shared with you: you leave them, and they keep the board.
  • Your name, photo and comments on shared boards, and your list of shared boards.
  • Your BusyAnts sign-in. Your Google account itself isn't touched.
  • The copy of your boards kept on the device you delete from.

Feedback you sent isn’t deleted when you delete your account in BusyAnts: it is kept apart from it. Email the address above to have it deleted, within 30 days of your request (asking by email to delete your account deletes your feedback too).

Backup files you downloaded stay on your own device until you delete them. Copies in the service's own safety backups, if any were made, are deleted as those backups expire, within 90 days. See the Privacy policy for everything BusyAnts stores.
