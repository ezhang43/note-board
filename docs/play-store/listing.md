## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
BusyAnts is a calm, free visual board for your notes, checklists and plans.

Think of an endless canvas. Drop notes and checklists anywhere, drag them where they belong, and see everything at once. No rigid pages, no clutter.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

- Add notes and checklists, and move them around the board.
- Group cards in containers that hold your notes and checklists together.
- Nest checklist items and tick them off.
- Put boards inside boards, for example one board per trip, project or class.
- Change card and container colors, and make text bigger or smaller.
- Search across all of your boards.
- Use light or dark mode.

PLAN TOGETHER

Share a board with a link. Anyone you share it with can sign in with Google and work on the same board with you, in real time. You can see who has the board, and you can turn the link off whenever you like.

SAVED AND SYNCED

Sign in with Google and your boards are saved online, so they open on every device you use. Changes you make without internet are kept on your device and saved when you are back online.

VERSION HISTORY

BusyAnts keeps earlier versions of your boards as you work. Look at an old version, or restore it if you change your mind.

STAY FOCUSED

- A built-in focus timer (25 minutes of focus, short breaks, a longer break after four rounds) with a soft chime.
- Google Calendar beside your board, if you choose to connect it. See your week or month, and add or change simple events without leaving BusyAnts.
- A Completed panel for the checklist items you have cleaned up.

MADE FOR PHONES AND TABLETS TOO

On a phone, BusyAnts has its own touch layout: a bottom bar for undo, redo and adding cards, and a menu for everything else. Pinch with two fingers to zoom, and press and hold a card to drag it.

YOURS TO KEEP

- Download a backup of all your boards in one file, any time.
- Bring in your notes from other apps: Markdown, text, Trello and Google Keep exports, and PDFs.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

Your boards are private to you unless you share one. There are no ads and no tracking cookies, and your data is never sold. Read the full privacy policy on our website.

BusyAnts is made by one person, and is free to use. If you like it, there is a Buy me a coffee link in the app.

Questions or ideas? Write to ezhang43@gmail.com.
```
