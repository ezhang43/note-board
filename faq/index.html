Questions and answers

Is BusyAnts free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no limit on how many cards, columns or boards you make. There are no ads. Online, all of your own boards together can hold about 900,000 characters of text, which is a lot of notes. If you'd like to help keep it going, see the last question.

Where are my boards saved?

In two places. A copy is kept on your device, so your boards open quickly and work without internet. The other copy is kept online with your Google account, in Google Firebase, so your boards open on every device you sign in on. Only you can see your boards, unless you share one.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once you've opened BusyAnts online on a device, it opens there without internet too. Changes you make offline are kept on the device and saved online when you're back. A small note at the bottom right says "Saving…", "Saved" or "Offline".

How does sharing work, and who can see a shared board?

Open a board and press Share, then Share this board, and send the link. Anyone who opens the link and signs in with Google gets that board, and every board inside it, and can change everything on it. Changes reach everyone within a second or two. The people a board is shared with can see each other's Google name and photo, never your email address, and anyone who has the board can copy the link and pass it on.

Only the person who shared the board can turn the link off, remove someone, rename it or delete it for everyone. Your home board, and boards you haven't shared, stay private.

How do I get a board back?

Press Ctrl+Z (⌘Z on a Mac) to undo a recent change. For anything older, open Version history (the clock button at the bottom right; on a phone, ⋯ then Version history). It keeps your newest 100 versions, saved as you work. Pick one to look at it, then press Restore this version. You can also keep your own copy at any time with File, then Download backup.

How do I delete my account and data?

Today you can delete any board except your home board from the Boards menu, delete cards and columns, leave a board someone shared with you, and delete a board you shared (it goes for everyone). There isn't a button to delete your whole account yet. To have your account and everything saved online deleted, email ezhang43@gmail.com; it is done within 30 days. To remove the copy on a device, sign out, then clear this site's data in your browser's settings.

Which browsers and phones work?

Recent versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on a computer, Safari on iPhone and iPad, and Chrome on Android phones and tablets. On a phone, BusyAnts uses a layout made for small screens. Signing in opens a small Google window, so allow pop-ups for this site if your browser blocks them.

How do I install it as an app?

In Chrome or Edge on a computer, open BusyAnts and press Install in the address bar. On an iPhone or iPad, open it in Safari, press Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome and choose Install app (or Add to Home screen) from the menu. The installed app has its own window and icon, works offline, and updates itself.

Can I import from other apps?

Yes, from Milanote. In Milanote, export a board as Markdown (.md). Then in BusyAnts choose File, then Import from Milanote, and pick the file. Its headings become checklists and its text becomes notes, added to the board you have open. You can also bring back a BusyAnts backup with File, then Restore from backup.
