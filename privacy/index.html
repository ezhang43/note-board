Privacy policy

Effective from October 19, 2026

The short version
  • BusyAnts stores your boards and the few details from your Google account it needs to sign you in and show who you share a board with, plus any feedback you choose to send.
  • Your boards are private to you, unless you share one.
  • No ads, and your data is never sold or given to anyone for marketing. The only thing counted is how many times each page is visited, with a cookie-free counter (see “Counting visits” below). Your boards are never part of it.

Who runs BusyAnts

BusyAnts is made and run by BusyAnts, in the United States. Questions about this policy or your data: ezhang43@gmail.com.

What is stored when you sign in

From your Google account

You sign in with Google. Google tells BusyAnts your name, email address, profile photo address and an account number. Firebase Authentication keeps these for your account. Your email address is only used to sign you in: it isn't shown to anyone you share a board with.

Your boards

Everything on your boards (names, cards, containers, checklist items, links, colors, sizes and positions), stored online so your boards open on every device you sign in on. With it are kept the time of the last save and a random number for the browser tab that saved it.

Version history

Earlier copies of your boards, saved automatically as you work, so you can look at or restore an old version. The newest 100 are kept; older ones are deleted.

Shared boards

When you share a board, or open a board shared with you, these are stored with it: the board and the boards inside it, who shared it, the current link, and a list of the people who have it, with each person's Google name, profile photo address, when they joined and the link they joined with. Your own account also keeps a list of the shared boards you have. Everyone a board is shared with can see everything on that board and the names and photos of the others who have it.

Feedback you send

If you send feedback (the Feedback button; on a phone, ⋯ then Feedback), what you wrote is stored with your email address, your account number and the time you sent it, so the maker can read it and reply to you. Nothing else is sent with it.

What is kept on your device

A copy of your boards, your version history and your sign-in, so BusyAnts opens quickly and works offline; which board is open and where you were on it; your light or dark choice and focus timer settings; and a copy of the site's files for offline use. These stay in your browser and can be removed by clearing this site's data in your browser's settings.

Google Calendar

If you open the calendar panel, Google asks whether BusyAnts may see and change your calendar events. If you agree, BusyAnts reads your events to show them, and adds, changes or deletes an event only when you do it in the panel. If you add guests to an event, their email addresses go to Google Calendar with it, and Google emails your guests about a change only when you choose Send. Google's permission lasts about an hour. It is kept in this browser on this device until it runs out (so reloading the page, opening another tab or reopening the browser within that hour doesn't ask Google again), and is dropped when you sign out, when someone else signs in, when you delete your account, or when it runs out. Your events are never saved with your boards or anywhere else by BusyAnts. You can take the permission back at any time in your Google account's settings.

The Claude connector (optional)

BusyAnts has an optional connector that lets Claude read your boards and add to them. It is a small program that runs on your own computer, and only if you install it. The BusyAnts site and app work the same without it.

  • It signs in with your own Google account, in your web browser, and works as you: it can reach your own boards and the boards shared with you (those only to read). The sign-in is kept in a file on that computer. Asking Claude to sign you out of BusyAnts deletes that file.
  • It reads or changes your boards only when you ask Claude to. In the connector you install, deleting is switched off.
  • What Claude reads from your boards goes to Claude, which is run by Anthropic, and is handled under Anthropic's privacy policy, not this one.
  • BusyAnts stores nothing new because of it. A change Claude makes is saved on your board like one you make, with a safety copy saved to your version history before its first change. The connector's list of its changes stays on your computer.

Counting visits

The published site counts page visits (the app and the About, FAQ, Terms, Privacy and Delete your account pages) with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter that uses no cookies and does no personal tracking. When a page opens, your browser asks GoatCounter to add one to that page's count. BusyAnts gives it only the page's address without anything after a “?” (so a share link's key is never sent). Like any website you load something from, GoatCounter also receives your IP address and browser details, and the page you came from. What is on your boards, and your account details, are never sent to it. It runs only on the published site, not on a copy run on a developer's computer.

Pictures and videos from web addresses

If a link card's web address is a picture or video (one you pasted or dragged in, or one on a board shared with you or imported), BusyAnts loads it straight from that website (or from YouTube or Vimeo for their players) whenever the card is shown, so that website can see that it was viewed, along with your IP address and browser details. Only the address is saved with your board; nothing is uploaded to BusyAnts.

Where it is kept, and who helps run it
  • Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, run by Google) stores your account details, boards and any feedback you send, in Cloud Firestore in australia-southeast1 (Sydney, Australia). Google also handles sign-in data in its own data centers. BusyAnts is run from the United States and your data is stored in Australia, so it may be processed outside your own country.
  • GitHub Pages (run by GitHub) serves the site's pages and files. Like any web host, it receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads.
  • Google Calendar (run by Google) holds your calendar events, if you use the calendar panel; BusyAnts reads and changes them there and keeps no copy.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so Google also receives your IP address when the fonts load.
  • GoatCounter counts page visits (see “Counting visits” above).
  • Anthropic (Claude), only if you use the optional Claude connector, receives what Claude reads from your boards when you ask it to (see “The Claude connector” above).

Each of these follows its own privacy policy. BusyAnts uses no other services and no advertising.

Who can see your data

Only you can read your own boards and version history; the security rules on the database turn everyone else away. A shared board can be read and changed by everyone it is shared with. Feedback you send can’t be read by anyone through BusyAnts, including you; only the maker reads it, in the Firebase console. The maker can technically see stored data in the Firebase console, and only looks when you ask for help or the law requires it.

How long it is kept

Your boards and version history are kept for as long as you use BusyAnts. A board you delete leaves your online copy at the next save, though it may stay in your version history until it becomes older than the newest 100 versions. A shared board deleted by the person who shared it is deleted for everyone. Feedback you send is kept until the maker deletes it; to have yours deleted sooner, email ezhang43@gmail.com and it will be deleted within 30 days of your request.

When you delete your account, everything stored online for it is deleted straight away: your boards, version history, the boards you shared (for everyone), your name and photo on shared boards, and your sign-in. Feedback is the exception: it is kept apart from your account, so deleting your account in BusyAnts doesn’t remove it; email to have it deleted, as above (asking by email to delete your account deletes your feedback too). Copies in the service's own safety backups, if any were made, are deleted as those backups expire, within 90 days.

Your choices
  • See and download everything: File, then Download backup.
  • Delete boards, cards and containers, leave a board shared with you, or delete a board you shared.
  • Delete your account and everything stored online except feedback you sent, at any time: in BusyAnts, File, then Delete account… (on a phone, ⋯, then File). If you can't sign in, email ezhang43@gmail.com and your account and everything stored online for it, feedback included, will be deleted within 30 days of your request. How to delete your account.
  • Ask to see, correct, delete or get a copy of your data by emailing ezhang43@gmail.com. Some US states give their residents extra privacy rights, and BusyAnts honors requests made under them.

Children

BusyAnts is not meant for children under 13 (or the minimum age for a Google account where you live).
